The platform

The quality processes you run every day, connected

Records link to each other, so an NCR can raise a CAPA, a failed gauge shows which inspections it touched, and an audit finding follows through to closure.

Nonconformance

Capture, contain and disposition nonconforming product

Log an NCR from the line, from receiving or from a customer complaint. Record containment, assign an owner and take it through disposition with signatures where they matter.

  • Severity, source, part, lot and site on every record
  • Raise a CAPA straight from the NCR
  • Files, history and approvals kept with the record
CAPA

Corrective action that is verified, not just closed

Write the problem statement, record the root cause with 5 Whys or another method, plan actions and set an effectiveness check, so the problem stays fixed.

  • Containment, root cause, actions and effectiveness in one place
  • Due dates and owners for every action
  • Verified-by and closed-on recorded automatically
PPAP and APQP

Part approval packages, element by element

Choose a submission level and the 18 PPAP elements are laid out with what to submit and what to retain. Track each element through to submission, and plan launches with APQP, FMEA and control plan documents.

  • Level 1 to 5 matrix built in
  • Package index and route for review
  • APQP projects, process FMEA and control plans

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can we host QualGuard IQ on our own servers?

Yes. It runs with Docker Compose on a Linux or Windows server inside your network.

Does using QualGuard IQ make us ISO 9001, IATF 16949 or AS9100 certified?

No software can certify you. QualGuard IQ keeps the records and workflows those standards expect, which makes preparing for and passing an audit easier.

Can we bring in our existing records?

Yes. Lists can be imported from Excel, and industry packs give you ready-made forms to start from.

Who decides what each person can do?

Your administrator. Permissions are assigned to roles in the admin area, and every change is kept in the audit trail.

Can records be deleted?

Only by people given that permission. Deletions are recorded in the audit trail.

How are updates delivered?

Tested updates are released on a maintenance plan. Your IT team installs them on your servers when it suits you.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Get in touch and we will set one up for you.
